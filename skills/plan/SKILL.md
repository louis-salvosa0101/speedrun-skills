# Plan the challenge

Read the challenge spec and inspect the project enough to make its tasks fit the existing stack and codebase. Make reasonable assumptions and record them in `PLAN.md`; never ask clarifying questions.

Write `PLAN.md` with:

- **MUST-HAVE**: at most 10 checkbox tasks, ordered by build dependency and implementation sequence.
- **NICE-TO-HAVE**: at most 5 checkbox tasks, ordered by value.
- **Assumptions**: concise decisions made where the spec was silent or ambiguous.

Stop after writing the plan. Do not implement tasks.
